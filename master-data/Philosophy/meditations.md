## What it actually is

Not a treatise — a working journal. Read it as someone rehearsing the same few
ideas until they became reflexes.

## Lines I return to

> You have power over your mind, not outside events. Realize this, and you will
> find strength.

- Separate the event from the story about the event.
- Most urgency is borrowed from other people's calendars.
- Do the work in front of you, at the quality you would want to be remembered for.

## How I use it

One page in the morning, no highlighting. If a line survives the day in my head,
it goes in this file.
