## Why I picked this up

I kept noticing that most "process problems" at work were really **structure**
problems. This book gave me vocabulary for that intuition.

## Core ideas

- A system is elements + interconnections + a purpose. Change the purpose and
  you change everything downstream.
- Stocks buffer flows. Delay between a decision and its effect is the source of
  most oscillation.
- Reinforcing loops grow; balancing loops resist. Real systems are tangles of both.

> The least obvious part of the system, its function or purpose, is often the
> most crucial determinant of the system's behavior.

## Leverage points, weakest to strongest

1. Numbers (subsidies, taxes, standards)
2. Buffer sizes
3. Delays
4. Information flows
5. Rules and incentives
6. Goals
7. Paradigms

## Notes to self

Whenever I am tempted to tune a number, ask first whether an information flow is
missing. Fixing *who knows what, when* is usually cheaper and lasts longer.
